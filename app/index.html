About this guide

Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 trophy list for Grand Theft Auto VI. Every guide on this page is written in our own words from those official descriptions and the game's own systems — nothing is copied from another guide. Where a guide draws on community knowledge, its sources are listed on the trophy itself (official PS5 trophy list, PSNProfiles, Exophase, PowerPyx). Difficulty and missable flags are our own judgement from the official descriptions: story trophies unlock by playing the story, and only a handful of one-off moments can be missed — those are flagged, with a save-or-replay note.

Progress is stored only in this browser (localStorage). Export it to keep a backup, import it to restore it on another machine.
